web3

I think this is what pulled me into computing.

I had always been on the computer messing around, but I remember in 6ème hearing about the Shiba Inu crypto.

I already knew Bitcoin, but SHIB sold me a dream at the time. I was a kid and I thought I would get rich.

I got interested, then I landed on Ethereum, and in my memory that is when the big NFT hype hit. Bored Ape, Mutant Ape, all of that.

I really loved that part of the ecosystem. It mixed things I liked: crypto, and something close to collectibles.

Then I started looking deeper into how a blockchain actually works, Ethereum in particular.

I wrote my first smart contracts in 4ème, then I completely stopped until première, because AI helped me a lot to learn in a field that is poorly documented in French, and I did not speak English very well then.

I really progressed thanks to that, and now I spend a lot of time on on-chain data: pulling it, processing it, and exposing it as fast as possible, especially for trading engines.
