Sport

I do a lot of sport.

I loved boxing when I was younger, but I stopped because the diet was too strict, and I wanted to try other sports.

Right now I train several: weightlifting (my current favorite), long runs, 100m sprint, climbing, and swimming.

I would like to add cycling and hiking later.

I prefer doing several sports. It lets me practice what I want when I want, discover different worlds, different ways of feeling effort, and it makes my body feel functional, not only aesthetic.
