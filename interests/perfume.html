Perfume

I love perfume for a lot of reasons.

I like trying to pick out every note.

I like having a scent that is not common.

I like being taken back by old smells and the memories they bring.

I do not only care about what I wear on my skin.

I also like ambient fragrance.

It really changes a room.

I grow my collection whenever I get the chance.

I am not especially into scents that are only big because of the buzz.

I will not buy something just because everyone likes it.

I would rather buy unknown perfumes that I actually love, even if I get fewer compliments, than stick to simpler, safer smells.
